Nock — dummy-style how to use it
================================

This is the "I have never installed an addon" version.
If a word sounds fancy, skip it and just do the numbered steps.


What Nock is
------------
Nock is a helper for Hunter characters in World of Warcraft: Forever,
especially Beast Mastery and Marksmanship.

It does four things:

1. Puts your hunter spells on easy keyboard keys (1, 2, 3, and so on).
2. Shows a little overlay that labels those keys, like cheat-sheet stickers
   on your keyboard.
3. When you train a new hunter spell, it explains what Forever changed
   about that spell, in plain chat text.
4. It has a written talent guide: what to pick while you level, and what
   to respec into at 60 for more damage.

It does NOT play the game for you.
It will not press keys, pick targets, or watch a fight and say
"press Aimed Shot now." That would break Blizzard's addon rules.
You still aim, you still press the buttons, you still decide.


New in 2.1: the ergonomic keymap and your mouse
-----------------------------------------------
2.1 moves a few keys so you can kite with your left hand on WASD, and
puts three spells on your mouse (on by default):

  Mouse 4 (side, back)    Wing Clip (it was Shift-Z; Shift-Z is now empty)
  Mouse 5 (side, front)   Concussive Shot (Q still works)
  Wheel click             Feign Death (E still works)
  Shift + Mouse 4 / 5     Strider Kick / Scatter Shot, once you have them

Scrolling the wheel stays camera zoom. Left and right click are never
touched. A mouse button that already does something (yours, or a
Blizzard default) is left alone; then Wing Clip stays on Shift-Z.
Careful: a hard scroll can click the wheel, and Feign Death has a
cooldown. Options > "Mouse buttons (Wing Clip, Concussive, Feign)"
turns the mouse off (Wing Clip goes back to Shift-Z); /nock mouse off
does the same.

The first time you log in on 2.1 a small window asks once:
  Move my keys     move Nock's keys to 2.1 and use the mouse
  Not now          change nothing (the red ! stays on the key bar;
                   click it, or type /nock migrate, to get the window back)
  Keep mouse off   move the keyboard keys only
Only keys that still hold Nock's own spell move. Keys you set yourself
stay yours. "Restore my old keys" in Options undoes all of it.
/nock keys lists every key.

The 2.1 keys:
  1-6         Open Fire, Arcane (Hawk at BM 60), Aimed, Multi, Serpent, melee
  Shift-1..6  Rapid Fire, Arcane/Trueshot, Sniper (MM), Volley, Scorpid, Lacerate
  Alt-1..5    racial, Distracting, Tranquilizing, Flare, Viper Sting
  Q / Shift-Q / Alt-Q / Ctrl-Q   Concussive / Scatter / Strider Kick / Scare Beast
  E / Shift-E / Alt-E / Ctrl-E   Feign Death / Deterrence / racial / Disengage
  R / Shift-R / Alt-R            Hunter's Mark / Beast Lore / Eagle Eye
  F / Shift-F / Alt-F / Ctrl-F   Freezing / Frost / Immolation / Explosive trap
  T / Shift-T / Alt-T / Ctrl-T   Pet attack / follow / stay / Feed Pet
  G / Shift-G / Alt-G / Ctrl-G   Mend Pet / Bestial Wrath / Intimidation / Call-Revive
  X / Shift-X / Alt-X / Ctrl-X   Hawk / Cheetah / Monkey / Pack
  Z / Ctrl-Z                     Aspect of the Wild / Aspect of the Beast
  H / Shift-H                    Eyes of the Beast / Tame Beast
  Shift / Alt / Ctrl-C           Track Beasts / Humanoids / Hidden
  C and V are Blizzard's again (character pane, nameplates).
  Ctrl-1..0 stay Blizzard's pet bar (Ctrl-8/9/0 = Aggressive/Defensive/Passive).
A key only gets its spell once you have learned it.


The cheat sheet (new in version 1.9)
------------------------------------
Click the Nock minimap button. A small card pops up. That's your whole
rotation in a few big lines. For a level-14 Beast Mastery hunter:

  R then 1   Mark it, then start the fight
  2          Press every time it's off cooldown
  Q / M5     It's running at me: slow it         (Concussive Shot)
  M4         It reached me: clip it, walk away   (Wing Clip, mouse button 4)

Later you also get:
  4          2+ mobs: press this one instead     (Multi-Shot, level 18)
  E / MMB    Oh no: play dead                    (Feign Death, level 30)

The card only shows spells you know, with the keys they are on right
now. "+ More detail" under it opens the long lists. The My Rotation tab
in the Nock window has the same cheat sheet on top, with a "More detail"
button for everything else.

The key strip (the "Nock  BM Leveling" bar with 1-6 and Q-T) is clickable
since 1.9: click a tile and it casts, exactly like pressing that key.
Hover a tile to see the spell and "Click or press 2". Grey tiles are
spells you haven't learned yet; they do nothing. Wing Clip gets its own
tile (M4, or Shift-Z with the mouse off) once you learn it at 12.
A small red "!" on a tile means that key isn't bound to what the tile
says. The bottom line then reads "Some keys aren't set - click Set me up";
click the small Set me up button next to it (out of combat).
In a fight the strip stays where it is: you can't drag, hide or change it
until the fight is over (Blizzard's rule for clickable buttons).

Wing Clip moved from Shift-6 to Shift-Z in 1.9, and to Mouse 4 in 2.1.
If you put something else on those keys yourself, Nock leaves it alone.


The easy way (new in version 1.8)
---------------------------------
1. Log in on your hunter.
2. A small window pops up: "Nock: Set me up". It already picked a
   profile for you (BM Leveling below 60). If you want a different one,
   click the small "change" link next to its name.
3. Click the big "Nock: Set me up" button. That's it. Your keys, your
   action bar and Nock's macros are done, and one line in chat says so.
   (In a fight? It waits until the fight is over.)
4. From now on Nock keeps up by itself: when you level up, train a new
   spell or take a talent, it puts the new thing on its key and says
   so in chat, like "Nock: Multi-Shot is now on 4."
   If you move a key yourself, Nock leaves that key alone and tells you
   once. Turn this off with /nock keepup.
5. When you have a talent point, a gold "Spend talent point" button
   shows up under the minimap (or under the card). Click it.
   Turn it off with /nock remind.

Click the Nock minimap button to show or hide the cheat sheet card.
Right-click it to open the Nock window. The window shows only My
Rotation and Talents; everything else is on the "More" tab.
/nock advanced shows all tabs again; /nock simple goes back.
Lost the popup? Type /nock setup.


What you need
-------------
- World of Warcraft: Forever (the Forever client, not Retail or Classic).
- A Hunter character. Nock will politely refuse on other classes.
- The Nock folder from the zip (the folder that has Nock.toc inside it).


Install, like you have never done this before
---------------------------------------------
1. Close World of Warcraft completely. Not just the character screen —
   quit the game.

2. Unzip Nock.zip. You should see a folder named Nock. Open it.
   Inside you must see files named Nock.toc and Nock.lua.
   If you see another folder named Nock inside Nock, keep going until
   you find the folder that actually contains Nock.toc. THAT folder
   is the one you install.

3. Find your Forever addons folder.

   Windows, typical path:

     World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns

   That sits next to your Retail folder. It is _forever_ with
   underscores, not the Retail folder.

   Mac, typical path:

     /Applications/World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns

   If the Interface or AddOns folders are missing, create them.
   The names must match exactly: Interface, then AddOns.

4. Copy the Nock folder into AddOns so the final path looks like:

     ...\Interface\AddOns\Nock\Nock.toc

   Not:

     ...\Interface\AddOns\Nock\Nock\Nock.toc

   and not loose files dumped straight into AddOns.

5. Start World of Warcraft: Forever.

6. At the character-select screen, click AddOns (usually bottom-left).
   Find Nock in the list and make sure it is checked / enabled.

7. If the game says an addon is out of date, check the box that says
   something like "Load out of date AddOns." Then enable Nock anyway
   and tell your friend who shared this (or me) the number you get from
   typing this in chat later:

     /dump select(4, GetBuildInfo())

8. Log in on your Hunter.


First time in game — the 60-second setup
----------------------------------------
1. Make sure you are NOT in combat. Stand in town. Do not start this
   in the middle of a fight. Nock is only allowed to change keys when
   you are out of combat.

2. Optional but smart: press Escape, open Key Bindings, and turn ON
   "Character Specific Key Bindings" if you also play other classes
   on this account. That way Nock only changes keys on this hunter,
   not on your mage.

3. Type this in chat and press Enter:

     /nock

4. The Nock window opens. It looks like any Blizzard window: your hunter
   portrait in the corner and six tabs along the bottom (Profiles,
   My Rotation, Talents, Rotation, Macros, Field Manual). It starts on
   Profiles.

   You will see four cards. Click one:
   - BM Leveling or BM 60 if your pet is the star.
   - MM Leveling or MM 60 if you want to be the big ranged shooter
     (Sniper Shot, Trueshot Aura, and so on).
   - Not sure? Click Detect at the bottom. Nock picks the card that
     matches your talent points and level. It only picks; it does not
     apply anything.

5. Under the cards is a keyboard preview: 1-6 and Q-T with a spell icon
   and a little gold keycap on each. Bright ones are spells you know.
   Grey ones are spells you have not learned yet; Nock fills those keys
   in later when you train them. Hover any of them for details.

6. Click "Apply Profile" (bottom right).

7. Chat will say how many keys it bound. The keyboard card should appear
   near the bottom of the screen with key labels like 1 Open Fire,
   3 Aimed Shot, 4 Multi-Shot. Drag it if it sits on top of your bars.
   Your action buttons also get a small gold keycap in the corner that
   shows which key casts that button.

That is it. Your keyboard is set up.


What the important keys do
--------------------------
Shared (both specs)

  (2.1 layout; the full table is in "New in 2.1" at the top.)
  1         Open Fire — starts Auto Shot and sends your pet.
            This is your "start shooting" button from level 1.
  3         Aimed Shot (you learn this around level 20)
  4         Multi-Shot (around level 18) — use this on groups
  5         Serpent Sting (around level 4)
  6         Melee: Counterattack, Mongoose Bite or Raptor Strike
  Q         Concussive Shot — slows something running at you (also Mouse 5)
  E         Feign Death — drop combat / drop threat (also wheel click)
  R         Hunter's Mark
  F         Freezing Trap
  Shift-F   Frost Trap
  Alt-F     Immolation Trap
  Ctrl-F    Explosive Trap
  G         Mend Pet
  T         Pet: attack what your mouse points at (or your target)
  Shift-T   Pet: follow       Alt-T  Pet: stay
  Ctrl-G    Call Pet, or Revive Pet if the pet is dead
  Ctrl-T    Feed Pet — keep the pet happy (green). Happy pets hit harder.
  Shift-X   Aspect of the Cheetah — run faster. Turn it off before a fight.
            You get dazed if you get hit while this is on.
  X         Aspect of the Hawk — your damage aspect (Monkey before 10)
  Alt-X     Aspect of the Monkey (dodge)
  Shift-1   Rapid Fire
  Shift-4   Volley
  Mouse 4   Wing Clip — melee slow, then walk away (Shift-Z if the mouse is off)
  Ctrl-E    Disengage — in Forever this dumps threat, it is not a leap

Marksmanship extras

  2         Arcane Shot
  Shift-2   Trueshot Aura
  Shift-3   Sniper Shot

Beast Mastery extras

  2         Summon Hawk (or Arcane Shot before you have Hawk)
  Shift-2   Arcane Shot once you have Hawk
  Shift-G   Bestial Wrath
  Alt-G     Intimidation

Dummy combat reminder (you still choose the buttons):
- Press 1 to start shooting and send the pet.
- Stand 8 to 35 yards away. Closer than 8 yards is the "dead zone."
  Your bow sulks. Hit them with 6, slow them with Mouse 4 (Wing Clip), and walk
  backwards until Auto Shot starts again.
- One enemy: Aimed Shot (3) is your heavy shot once you have it.
- Several enemies: Multi-Shot (4) instead. 3 and 4 share a cooldown,
  so you pick one, you do not smash both.
- Beast Mastery with hawks: Hawk, Hawk, then Arcane Shot.
- Keep the pet fed (Ctrl-T) and healed (G). A dead pet is a sad hunter.


The talent guide (version 1.1; since 1.4 it shows your profile's plan)
--------------------------------------------
Nock will not spend talent points for you. It just tells you what
community guides recommend right now, in writing.

Open it any of these ways:

  - Type /nock guide
  - Or open /nock and click "Talent guide"
  - Type /nock next if you only want "what do I take at my level?"

Inside the manual, click Beast Mastery or Marksmanship.

- Beast Mastery shows the easy leveling path (33/18/0), point by
  point, and the level-60 damage respec (31/20/0).
- Marksmanship shows a path that finishes as the 20/31/0 damage
  build, with Sniper Shot at 60.

When you ding from level 10 onward, Nock also prints the next
recommended talent in chat. That is a reminder, not an order.
These builds came from public Forever guides in early November 2026.
The meta can move. If a trusted guide you like disagrees later,
trust newer research.

Dummy version of "what is a respec?"
At 60, Beast Mastery leveling talents are not identical to the
dungeon/raid damage talents. You visit a class trainer, pay a bit
of gold, wipe your points, and follow the 31/20/0 list in the manual.


Priority glow and opener numbers (new in version 1.7)
-----------------------------------------------------
Two marks on your own action buttons, taken from My Rotation:

  Priority glow    a soft gold glow on the buttons for your top 5
                   single-target spells. #1 is brightest, then each one
                   is a little dimmer.
  Opener numbers   small blue 1, 2, 3 tags on the left edge of the
                   buttons for your opener, in the order to press them.

They are set out of combat and do NOT change during a fight. They don't
light up when something is ready; they only show what matters most. They
move by themselves (after the fight, if you're in one) when you move a
spell on your bars, change keys, learn a spell, level up or change
profile.

Options on the Profiles tab: Priority glow, Opener numbers, Glow style
(Steady or Pulse, a slow fade that is the same on every button) and
Intensity (Low, Medium, High). Both are on by default.
  /nock glow                 glow on or off
  /nock glow steady | pulse  glow style
  /nock glow low | medium | high
  /nock numbers              opener numbers on or off

Works on Blizzard's bars, and on Bartender4, Dominos and ElvUI bars when
Nock can find their buttons.

Automatic updates and chat lines (new in 3.10.2)
------------------------------------------------
You do not need to click Rebuild after training. About a second and a half
after the last learn event, and never during a fight, Nock reads the
spellbook, puts new spells on their planned EMPTY keys, repaints the bar and
prints what changed: "Added Feign Death to E", "Arcane Shot upgraded to
Rank 6". A trainer visit gives one grouped message. Pets: "Pet Rex learned
Bite (Rank 4), on pet bar slot 5" and "New pet Whiskers (Cat): ...". A key you
set yourself is never replaced. /nock announce off turns the chat lines off
(keys are still filled); /nock announce on turns them back on.

Rebuild and /nock check (new in 3.10.1)
---------------------------------------
Trained something and don't see it? Click Rebuild on the My Rotation page,
or type /nock check. Nock rescans your spellbook (every rank, highest one
used), fills newly trained spells onto their planned EMPTY keys (a key you
set yourself is never replaced), repaints the bar and prints in chat:
how many spells it read, what is on a tile, what is on a key but has no tile,
what is on nothing, and which spells your trainer still has for you.
/nock check feign death (any spell name) shows how Nock sees one spell.

My Rotation — your own dummy guide (new in version 1.6)
-------------------------------------------------------
Type /nock myrotation (or click the My Rotation tab). It reads three
things, only when you are OUT of combat: your level, the spells in your
spellbook, and which keys and action-bar buttons those spells are on.
Then it writes the guide for the active profile:

  Opener          the buttons to press to start a fight, in order, each
                  with its icon and the key it is on right now
  Single target   numbered: press the highest one that's ready
  Two or three mobs / Four or more mobs
  Moving and kiting
  Oh no           Feign Death, traps, Intimidation, Disengage...
  Pet             call, mend, feed, attack, follow
  Coming soon     the next 1-3 things you get and how they change this
  Most damage tips

If a spell you should use has no key, it says "not bound" in red with a
Bind it button. Bind it puts the spell on the key Nock planned for it
(out of combat only; Restore Keys can undo it). Bind Missing does all of
them at once.

It updates by itself after a level-up, a new spell, a key or bar change,
a talent change or a profile change. If that happens in a fight, it waits
until the fight ends. It never watches your target, health, buffs,
cooldowns or mana. It is a printed guide, not a live helper.

The rotation card: type /nock card (or click Show Card) for a small card
with the same opener and priority. Drag it anywhere. The padlock (or
/nock card lock) locks it in place. Its X hides it.

When you level up, chat says what's new, for example:
  New at 18 (trainer): Multi-Shot on 4 — use it on 2+ mobs instead of
  Aimed Shot.
Remember to visit the trainer: you only get a spell after you buy it.

Profiles — one click (new in version 1.4, new window in 1.5)
------------------------------------------------------------
Type /nock profiles (or just /nock). Click a card:

  BM Leveling   31/20/0  Sanctum's Mobalytics leveling path, no respec,
                         no Summon Hawk (Arcane Shot is better since Nov 3)
  BM 60         31/20/0  wow-forever.gg raid/dungeon build, with
                         Summon Hawk, full Frenzy and Hawk Eye
  MM Leveling   7/31/13  LudiDeo's Mobalytics 1-60 path, keeps the pet,
                         Sniper Shot at 40
  MM 60         5/41/5   Staedt's Lone Wolf ranged build (no pet in
                         groups). Most published MM build

The four small icons at the top right of the window switch profile on
every tab. A green check marks the one that is active.

The tabs:
  Profiles      the cards, a keyboard preview, and options
                (keyboard card, action-button keycaps, chat tips,
                auto-binding newly trained spells)
  My Rotation   YOUR guide, new in 1.6: what to press at your level with
                the spells you know and the keys you have bound right now.
                See "My Rotation" below.
  Talents       the point-by-point plan as a list: a green check for
                learned, a gold arrow for your next point, a padlock
                for levels you have not reached. A bar shows x / 51.
  Rotation      a written field manual for that profile and level range,
                with gold [key] references. Read it, then you decide in
                the fight.
  Macros        every macro Nock makes, with its key, icon and text,
                plus extras. Click Copy to get the text highlighted for
                Ctrl+C.
  Field Manual  "What do I take next?", Forever notes for each spell
                (grey if you have not learned it yet) and the Auto Shot
                rules.

Buttons at the bottom (they change with the tab):

  Apply Profile   Out of combat: sets your keys, puts shots on bar 1
                  and creates the Nock macros below. It never spends
                  talent points.
  Restore Keys    Puts back your keys from before the first apply
                  (asks first).
  Learn Next      Talents tab: spends ONE point on the next talent.
  Learn All       Talents tab: spends every point you have, in plan
                  order (asks first). Handy right after a respec at 60.
  Open Talents    Opens Blizzard's talent window.

In combat these buttons turn grey and the bottom of the window says why.
They come back when combat ends. Esc closes the window, and it remembers
where you dragged it.

What Apply changes with a profile (Benjamin's layout):
  1  Open Fire macro: pet attack + Auto Shot (never toggles off)
  2  Arcane Shot macro (BM with Summon Hawk: Hawk on 2, Arcane on Shift-2)
  3  Aimed Shot macro   4  Multi-Shot macro   5  Serpent Sting macro
     (each one also makes sure Auto Shot keeps running)
  6  Counterattack / Mongoose Bite / Raptor Strike melee macro
  Q  Concussive Shot (plain spell)
  E  Feign Death macro: stop cast, stop attack, pet follow, Feign
  R  Leveling profiles: Hunter's Mark + send pet.  60 profiles: plain Mark
  F  Freezing Trap   Shift-F Frost   Alt-F Immolation   Ctrl-F Explosive
  T  pet attacks your mouseover (or target)   Shift-T follow   Alt-T stay
  Ctrl-G  Call Pet / Revive Pet
  Shift-1  Rapid Fire + both trinkets
  Shift-G  BM: pet attack + Bestial Wrath   Shift-3  MM: Sniper Shot
  Alt-G    BM: pet attack + Intimidation
  Mouse    Mouse 4 Wing Clip, Mouse 5 Concussive, wheel click Feign Death
           (only on empty buttons; /nock mouse off turns them off)

Each macro casts at most one spell on the global cooldown per press.
Nock needs up to 16 character macro slots. If you are full, it tells
you and changes nothing.

Chat commands:
  /nock profiles                       open the Profiles tab
  /nock profile bmlvl|bm60|mmlvl|mm60  apply one right now
  /nock learn                          learn the next talent in the plan
  /nock learnall                       learn every point you can

At 60, switching from a leveling profile to a 60 profile means paying a
trainer for a respec first, then clicking Learn All on the Talents tab.


Where else to find Nock
-----------------------
Click the addon button next to the minimap (the addon compartment) and
pick Nock. Left-click opens the window, right-click turns the keyboard
card on or off. If your client has no addon compartment, Nock puts its
own round button on the minimap instead.

If something looks wrong
------------------------
"It did nothing."
  Are you on a Hunter? Are you out of combat? Did you click Apply
  Profile? Type /nock again and look at chat for an error.

"It overwrote keys I liked."
  Click Restore Keys on the Profiles tab, or type /nock restore. That only remembers
  what the keys were before your first Apply. After that, it cannot
  time-travel further back, so be a little thoughtful before you
  Apply on a character with a custom keyboard you love.

"My mage's keys changed too."
  Turn on Character Specific Key Bindings, then set the mage back
  up (or restore on the hunter after enabling that option and
  re-applying only on the hunter).

"A spell is grey / missing."
  You have not trained it yet. Visit a hunter trainer. When you
  learn it, Nock should fill the empty reserved key and explain
  the spell in chat.

"The keyboard card is in the way."
  Drag it, click its small X, or type /nock overlay to hide it.
  (Out of combat only. In a fight it hides as soon as the fight ends.)

"My friend and I want the same buttons."
  Give them this same Nock folder. They install it the same way
  and click the same spec. That is the whole point — you can talk
  about keys in the same language ("stun is Shift-E").

Nock is free. Do not charge anyone for it. The code is readable on
purpose, which Blizzard requires.


What Nock will never do
-----------------------
It will not bot, it will not click for you, and it will not run a
live "best shot right now" helper. If you want a reminder of how
a spell works, use Field notes or wait until you train it. If you
want talent advice, use the Field Manual. The rest is you, a bow,
and (usually) a slightly judgmental pet.
